What does CTPL mean?

CTPL stands for Compulsory Third Party Liability. It concerns eligible third-party death and bodily injury claims arising from motor vehicle accidents, subject to policy terms and limits. Read the Insurance Commission guidance.

How does CTPL relate to registration?

CTPL is part of the motor vehicle registration framework. Confirm the policy period and the evidence of cover required for your specific registration or renewal. See the Insurance Commission circular in the Supreme Court E-Library.

Does CTPL cover damage to my vehicle?

CTPL focuses on eligible third-party death and bodily injury claims. Do not assume it pays for repairs to your own vehicle or third-party property damage. Ask the insurer to explain the policy’s boundaries and exclusions.

Can this website issue my CTPL policy?

This website helps you understand CTPL and prepare an enquiry in chat. It does not submit an application, take payment, issue a quote or certificate, or bind insurance cover. Confirm actual terms and issuance with the insurer.

How does the CTPL assistant use my messages?

The assistant uses OpenAI for general CTPL questions. Conversation messages and request status are saved in MySQL, with history linked to your current browser session. Keep sensitive personal and vehicle documents out of chat. The assistant cannot confirm coverage or registration.
